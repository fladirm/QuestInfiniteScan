## 1. Frozen semantic equation

### 1.1 World

\[
\boxed{\Psi:\Sigma_2\rightarrow S16}
\]

`Σ₂` is one signed-64 intrinsic carrier/gauge. It is not an atlas of physical
chart objects. Supported and disconnected manifestations are values/relations of
the field.

### 1.2 Canonical default

The authoritative program must prove a canonical no-manifestation representative

\[

\]

Only after sensor/eye/export no-manifestation proof and exact intrinsic-relation
parity between unbacked and allocated-default addresses may unbacked storage and
`NULL` codec blocks stand for this same value. A default/support boundary may have
defined native relation semantics. Current `ZNullDyad` plus `Gz=0` is not
sufficient evidence.

### 1.3 One relation-program authority

\[
\boxed{\mathfrak M=\operatorname{Compile}(I_{TOE},\mathcal A_{S16})}.
\]

`𝔐` is one fingerprinted IR with query entry points, not one monolithic kernel.
Every specialized plan inherits its expressions, arity, brackets, coupling,
default semantics and reverse contractor from this IR.

For any query:

\[
Y_q=\mathfrak M_q[\Psi].
\]

### 1.4 Scan

\[
Y_t=(O_L,O_R,\Gamma_t)
\]

is one coherent stereo observation. Depth and optical leaves per eye stay
independent until their reverse constraints meet.

\[
\mathcal A_q(O)=\{\Phi:\mathfrak M_q[\Phi]\in O\}.
\]

Alternative preimages remain a mathematical union and transient contractor
scratch. They are never physical branch/chart/object identities.

### 1.5 One close/commit

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{NativeCloseCommit}(\Psi_t,Y_t,\mathcal C_t)
}
\]

combines accumulated evidence, both sensor shadows and native relation predicates
before selection. It publishes only a uniquely resolved or complete-union-common
full-S16 delta.

If the current field remains feasible, canonical bytes remain unchanged.

Selector preservation is defined by equivalence fibres of the complete accumulated
Merkaba program. A kernel/right-lift optimization is legal only after a generated
complete-coupling proof.

ranges remain scratch.

native relation predicates.

Output:

```text

no-change evidence receipts

```

It is the physical lowering of the single semantic close.

### Commit stages

generations. Root exchange is the final visible instruction.

- a second modality-specific solver appears;
- old and replacement graphs both remain live;
- a run becomes production-LOC positive without an immediate deletion path;
- device failure would require changing native law outside the active cut.

The next action is then one precise blocker report, not a new architecture.
