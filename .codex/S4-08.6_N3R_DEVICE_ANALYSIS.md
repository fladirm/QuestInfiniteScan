## Cold-start and ring-exhaustion root causes

The original approximately 19-second start stall was caused by eagerly allocating
the full decoded-residency budget. N3 now treats that budget as a ceiling and
allocates only the initial two-page current/shadow pair.

The original failure after eight frames was not a mathematical unresolved limit.
The unresolved journal retained whole `StereoRigFrameLease` objects and therefore
held all RGB/depth ring textures. N3 now requests the small terminal readbacks,
releases the capture lease immediately after the GPU fence/readback issue and
retains one copied 272-byte exact unresolved record. Continuous revisions and the
successful stop/start interval prove that capture resources recycle.
